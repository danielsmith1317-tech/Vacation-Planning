# Vacation Planning

Permanent multi-trip vacation hub for the Smith family.

## Trip-page standard

Every trip page should carry forward the same core planning features used in the Paris & London planner where relevant:

- Overview and trip status
- Flights / trains / transportation using the detailed Paris/London-style travel cards
  - show departure/arrival dates and local times, carrier/train number, connections/layovers, and confirmed seat/class details when known
  - include Google Maps links for every departure/arrival airport and train station when available
- Hotels or rentals with direct official/listing links, map links, confirmed dates, room/benefit notes, and booking status when known
- Itinerary / things to do, including a dedicated tours/experiences section when relevant; distinguish research from confirmed bookings
- Map links and embedded map context
- Confirmed / planned booking links
- Budget / booking notes where applicable
- Live weather that loads automatically when the page opens
  - Future trips: current conditions + short-range forecast are planning context only until the trip enters forecast range
  - Archived trips: current conditions are reference weather, not historical trip weather

Use the existing authorized Google Maps / Weather setup when available and keep private booking-access details off the public site.

## Visual standard

- Use the Paris & London planner as the baseline for information hierarchy, cards, navigation, mobile behavior, and travel-detail density.
- Each trip may use its own accent/theme color so trips are visually distinct; keep the overall hub cohesive.
- Maps, weather, transportation endpoints, lodging links, and confirmed/planned status should be one tap away.
- Do not invent missing historical details. Add only known facts or clearly labeled research.

## Active-trip usability standard

Every active/future trip should also include, when the information exists:

- **Trip at a Glance** near the top with dates, travelers, lodging, transportation and the next open decisions.
- A visible **status strip** distinguishing confirmed items from TBD/research items.
- A **day-by-day itinerary** that becomes the operational center of the trip.
- A **Food & Groceries** area appropriate to the trip style. Rentals should highlight kitchen/self-catering value; all-inclusive trips should not over-prioritize off-site dining.
- A **Budget / Bookings dashboard** with confirmed vs TBD items, paid/remaining amounts and cancellation deadlines as those become known.
- Transportation cards should link every known airport and train station to Google Maps.
- Confirmed lodging should always have a direct official/listing link and a map link.
- Keep the site mobile-first and make the information needed during travel reachable in one or two taps.
