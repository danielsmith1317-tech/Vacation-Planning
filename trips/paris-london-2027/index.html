Decision View

Top choices are ranked within the filters you select above. Location now leads the ranking, with extra emphasis on being walkable to the trip sights and having strong Metro/transit access. Choosing Marriott, Hilton, IHG, Independent, a price cap, favorites, or access filter recalculates the finalists for that filtered set.
Finalist · top 5 Worth Considering · next 8 Archive · hidden from decision view
Budget rule: Hotels over $1,500 per room for the full stay are removed from the active hotel list. If only a combined 2-room quote is available, anything over $3,000 is removed because at least one room must exceed the cap.

Scoring: Location 50% · Price/value 16% · Family practicality 7% · Newness/renovation/condition 7% · Reviews 5% · Luxury/caliber 5% · Food & member perks 5% · Loyalty 3% · Flexibility 2%. Location is the strongest factor. Within Location, walking/sight proximity counts 65% and Metro/transit access counts 35%. Food & member perks favor included breakfast first, then verified elite/member lounge access and self-catering value. Rentals receive a grocery/self-catering benefit because the family can keep food in the unit and make breakfast; Wi-Fi is displayed but does not add ranking points. Marriott receives the strongest loyalty benefit because Daniel and Nicole can both use Marriott benefits; Hilton receives a smaller loyalty benefit for Daniel. Other brands receive no loyalty boost.
