What to Wear · Paris & London in June

Paris: neat, comfortable smart-casual pieces, breathable shirts, chinos/trousers, casual dresses or similar outfits; wear broken-in walking shoes rather than choosing footwear for appearance alone. Add a light layer and rain protection.

London: layers and a packable rain jacket are useful for changeable conditions. Comfortable shoes are essential for pavements, parks and transit. Pubs and casual restaurants generally do not require formal clothing; check any particular venue dress code.

Churches and special visits: dress respectfully; check specific site rules. For both cities, pack for actual forecast conditions shortly before departure rather than treating June averages as a forecast.
