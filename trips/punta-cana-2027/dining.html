Royalton Bávaro · Restaurants and Dining

On-resort choices for the family · 2027 trip · Ideas, not reservations

What to prioritize

Special dinners: Hunter for steak, Grazie for Italian, RIO for Brazilian grilled meats, and Zen for teppanyaki. Flexible family meals: Gourmet Marché and Oceanside. Easy snacks: Scoops and poolside grill options. No need to leave the all-inclusive resort just to find restaurants.

Diamond Club breakfast at Oceanside and Beach Club Grill access depend on eligible room benefits. Confirm reservations and current operating hours with the resort concierge; the times below are the resort's currently published hours and can change before your trip.

Diamond Club dining

Diamond Club Beach Club Grill: Diamond Club beachfront lunch and snacks, noon–5 p.m. Oceanside: breakfast 7–10:30 a.m. is Diamond Club only; its lunch and dinner are not listed as exclusive. Confirm access for each room at check-in.
